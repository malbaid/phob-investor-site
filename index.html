Every engagement initiative requires proactive mitigation planning to ensure smooth implementation and sustainable participation.

Low Participation

Some employees may initially hesitate to participate in hobby activities.

Mitigation: Strong communication campaign, gamification, and management support.

📌

Maintenance Challenge

The board and community activities require continuous updates.

Mitigation: Assign community PIC and scheduled monthly refresh activities.

💬

Communication Gap

Mitigation: Integrate PHOB into internal communication channels and events.

Program Sustainability

Long-term engagement consistency can become challenging.

Mitigation: Continuous innovation, events, and reward-based participation.
